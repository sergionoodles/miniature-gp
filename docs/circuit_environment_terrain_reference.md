# Formula 1 Circuit Environment & Terrain Reference

This table describes each circuit as a piece of terrain embedded in its environment, focusing on the information useful for visually reconstructing the location.

| # | Circuit | Setting | Terrain & elevation | Water / coast | Vegetation / ground cover | Built environment & landmarks | Terrain reconstruction instructions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Albert Park — Australia** | **Urban park** inside Melbourne, immediately south of the city centre | Very flat, gently undulating parkland; essentially no significant natural elevation | **Large artificial lake** occupies the interior of the park; Port Phillip Bay is relatively close to the south | Extensive manicured grass, mature scattered trees, park vegetation | Dense Melbourne skyline visible from parts of the circuit, but the immediate environment is predominantly open green parkland | **Build a large flat green urban park**, with the circuit looping around/alongside a large irregular lake. Use mature trees in clusters rather than continuous forest. Put Melbourne's high-rise skyline beyond the park, particularly toward the north. Avoid making it look like countryside. |
| **2** | **Bahrain International Circuit — Bahrain** | **Desert / isolated motorsport complex** in Sakhir | Very flat to gently rolling desert; artificially landscaped circuit complex | No meaningful adjacent water | Sparse desert vegetation; landscaped greenery immediately around facilities | Large purpose-built circuit complex, grandstands and distinctive control tower; otherwise huge open desert | **Start with an almost completely barren beige/tan desert plain.** Place the entire circuit as an isolated engineered complex surrounded by open sand/rocky desert. Add only sparse low vegetation. Keep the horizon extremely open. The circuit should feel like a man-made island of infrastructure in the desert. |
| **3** | **Jeddah Corniche — Saudi Arabia** | **Coastal urban street circuit** | Essentially flat coastal terrain | **Red Sea immediately alongside parts of the circuit** | Sparse/arid vegetation with landscaped coastal greenery | Jeddah urban development, corniche, hotels, roads and modern buildings | **Make this a flat Arabian coastal strip.** One side should clearly open toward the Red Sea, while the other contains modern Jeddah development. Use sandy/beige ground, palms and sparse landscaping. The sea should be visually dominant in sections, but don't turn it into a beach circuit—the track runs through developed Corniche infrastructure. |
| **4** | **Shanghai International Circuit — China** | **Suburban/peripheral planned motorsport complex** | Very flat, lowland terrain | Numerous **artificial ponds/water features** around the complex rather than a natural coastline | Very extensive manicured lawns and planted greenery | Huge grandstands and paddock complex; relatively sparse urban surroundings | **Create a huge flat landscaped green complex**, surrounded by Shanghai's relatively flat suburban/peripheral landscape. The area should feel deliberately planned and monumental. |
| **5** | **Suzuka International Racing Course — Japan** | **Large recreational resort / semi-rural setting** | Gently rolling, with noticeable elevation changes | No major water body immediately defining the circuit | **Lush green vegetation and trees**, with wooded areas | Suzuka Circuit amusement park, hotels and resort infrastructure | **Use lush Japanese green terrain**, with mature trees and wooded sections around the circuit. | **6** | **Miami International Autodrome — Miami** | **Stadium / suburban urban complex** | Flat South Florida terrain | Artificial marina/water features around the complex; **not directly oceanfront** | Tropical/subtropical landscaping, palms and lawns | **Hard Rock Stadium dominates the environment**, with parking lots, sports facilities and suburban development | **Do not make this a conventional isolated racetrack.** Build a very flat stadium campus. The environment should feel like a massive American sports complex rather than countryside. |
| **7** | **Imola — Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari** | **Urban-edge park / river valley** | **Rolling/hilly**, with meaningful changes in elevation | **Santerno River immediately alongside the circuit** in several areas | Mature trees, wooded sections and green riverbank | Imola residential development is relatively close; circuit sits between city and natural river landscape | **Place the circuit in a green river valley immediately adjacent to a medium-sized Italian city.** Put the Santerno as a clearly defined river corridor beside/near the track. Use dense mature trees and grassy embankments, with houses/buildings appearing beyond the greenery. Terrain should rise noticeably away from the river. |
| **8** | **Circuit de Monaco — Monaco** | **Extreme dense urban street circuit** | **Very steep Mediterranean hillside** | **Mediterranean Sea / harbour directly below and beside the circuit** | Minimal natural vegetation; ornamental palms and planted greenery | Extremely dense high-rise buildings, apartment blocks, hotels, old city, harbour, yachts | **Build a steep Mediterranean city climbing directly out of the sea.** Streets should be narrow and surrounded by buildings almost everywhere. | **9** | **Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya — Spain** | **Semi-rural/peripheral industrial countryside** near Barcelona | Gently rolling Catalan terrain | No significant water immediately adjacent | Mediterranean vegetation, grass, scattered trees | Circuit infrastructure, industrial estates and villages; Barcelona itself is not visually dominant | **Place the track in open Catalan countryside just outside an industrial/suburban zone.** Use dry-ish Mediterranean grass, scattered trees and low hills. | **10** | **Circuit Gilles-Villeneuve — Canada** | **Park circuit on an island** | Very flat artificial island | **St Lawrence River surrounds Île Notre-Dame** | Mature park trees, lawns and landscaped areas | Montreal skyline visible across the water; Olympic-era park infrastructure | **Create a flat, elongated green island inside a huge river.** Put the circuit through a park-like landscape of lawns and mature trees. ## Key Visual Distinctions

For procedural reconstruction, these distinctions are often more important than exact track geometry:

- **Monaco** → steep Mediterranean city carved into a mountainside above the sea
- **Baku** → flat Caspian waterfront + historic stone hill + modern city
- **Jeddah** → flat Red Sea coastal strip + modern Corniche
- **Zandvoort** → North Sea dune system + resort town
- **Gilles-Villeneuve** → flat artificial island + river on all sides + Montreal skyline
- **Albert Park** → flat urban park + large lake + skyscrapers
- **Monza** → historic enormous woodland/park surrounding the track
- **Imola** → green river valley immediately beside an urban area
- **Spa** → deep forested Ardennes hills
- **Red Bull Ring** → open Alpine valley with dramatic mountains behind
- **COTA** → rolling, relatively dry Texas hills
- **Bahrain/Lusail** → engineered motorsport facility surrounded by almost featureless desert
- **Yas Marina** → artificial island + marina + luxury resort
- **Miami** → American stadium campus rather than a city-centre circuit
- **Madrid** → modern exhibition/urban-development district rather than historic Madrid

> **Madrid note:** the 2026 MADRING is the new circuit around IFEMA and Valdebebas, rather than the old Jarama circuit. It combines public roads with permanent/private areas and is split between the IFEMA South and Valdebebas North zones.
